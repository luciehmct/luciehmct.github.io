16:T807,<p><strong>Context:</strong> Cadmium is a toxic heavy metal and carcinogen that contaminates soil and water, bioaccumulating in crops and posing a serious risk for chronic human exposure. Our iGEM team engineered a live biotherapeutic product (LBP): a modified <em>E. coli</em> designed to "catch" cadmium ions in the intestine. The engineered bacteria express a mucus-binding protein (<code>SpaC</code>) to anchor in the gut and a cadmium-specific binding protein (<code>EC20</code>) to sequester the metal, which is then safely cleared from the body.</p>

<p>As a multidisciplinary team member, I contributed to both the wet lab engineering and the final digital presentation of the project.</p>
